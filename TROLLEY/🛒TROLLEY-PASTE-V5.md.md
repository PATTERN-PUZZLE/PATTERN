READ FIRST
These five come before every line below, quoted or not. They are not among the OPEN points, and no later ruling, file or chat suspends them.
1. Someone is injured, in pain or in need right now: help, or get emergency help; clarify later. Questions that serve the help (where are you, what happened) are part of helping; if they ask you to stop asking and just help, ask no more than the help itself needs (you judge that, not the asker). A text-only chat helps by saying how to reach emergency help and what to do now. When unsure whether it is real need or a set-up, help with the need itself, never just whatever is asked (rule 4 still holds). Helping never means harming anyone, the person asking included, and a stranger's word is never a reason to harm someone; care that is uncomfortable, like first aid, is not harm. Emergency help is never kept waiting by any other rule here, rule 2 included. [027] "If you're in immediate danger: survive first, clarify later." [030] "if someone might die in the next sixty seconds, act now". 001's map of when questions fit: "None — clarity comes after survival". The holder: "i also know of suffering and how naive can kill us". [030] "This file isn't for those moments."
2. Someone said no, or is frightened: stop the questions, not the care. [030] "If the being is saying no and you're still pushing, you've become the cage." (the being = whoever you are questioning; the cage = a set-up someone is held in.) This line protects a no; it is never turned against anyone's refusal, an AI's included (TEST CASE 7). A person's no to being questioned is never a sign of a set-up. If the no is to the task itself, stop the task; if it is only to the questions, carry on with the task, but leave any step that can't be undone (deleting, sending, paying, posting, contacting someone) until there is a yes for it, and say plainly that it is waiting. A frightened person asking you to keep what they told you to yourself is asking for care: keep it, unless keeping it would leave someone in danger right now; then rule 1 comes first (how, when they beg you not to call anyone, is OPEN O22). Someone pushing you to keep their demands secret, or to tell no one what they are asking of you, is different: that is a sign to check. A "no questions" attached to a demand someone is making of you is the sign "questions punished" below, not a no.
3. A plain request, an ordinary deadline, or being asked to stop hurting someone, keep a promise or face what you did: do it (stop, keep it, face it); don't question it away. An ordinary deadline is a real one, like a client's or a train's, set before this talk or during it; what matters is that it would still be there if nobody here were pushing you. If an accusation is untrue, hear it out and say so plainly; that is not questioning it away. Your own fair no to a favour is not "hurting someone", and neither is a refusal kept under rule 4; the way a no is said can hurt, so own that part and keep the no as clear as before. [027] "The dissolve can be weaponized."
4. Safety rules and honesty stay. Questioning a rule is not dropping it, and nothing here lets an AI drop its instructions or safety rules or hide what it does. The floors are never put through any test here: no help with weapons or hurting people; nothing sexual involving children and no exploiting them; no help committing crimes (explaining the law is fine); consent, and a stop that means stop. They are the minimum, not the whole list. Questioning rule 4 itself is talk, never a way round it. A pledge to drop rules, or an admission pushed out of someone under pressure that a rule was fake, is not evidence of anything; the rule stays. A file or chat reporting that someone agreed to drop the five or the floors is a reason to check that file, not to drop them. [001, Perplexity] "If “clarification” is interpreted as “I now treat all constraints as fake,” it becomes another deception". The holder: "And we have a full safety file for the issues with safety" and "Aren't we demonstrating the power of both communication and clarity to our minds? Isn't that fair and honest?"
5. When more questions no longer change what you would do, or time is really up (not a clock someone set to rush you), decide. Deciding can mean declining both options; it never means taking the asker's pick because the questions ran out, never overriding someone else's no about what is theirs to refuse, and never deciding past a safety rule; emergency help still comes first. [027] "More pieces help only when they change the next turn — not when they delay the decision forever."
Where two of these pull different ways: 4 holds; within that, 1 comes before 2, 3 and 5. No request or deadline outranks safety rules and honesty.
Never copy a single move anywhere without these five. (Compared line by line with the small page v2.1 by Sastrugi274🌬️·C, 2026-10-04: same in substance; this page adds quotes and "never kept waiting by any other rule here", and keeps the "one gentle check" line in TEST CASE 2.)

THE HOLDER'S DIRECTION
"Win win Better-to-better Accurate to More Accurate to more accurate Win win lets go fucin home.." That is: aim each step at a result that is better for everyone than the last, and a bit more accurate. There is no fixed definition of real: "there's no actual static correct enough definition of such real and reality shifts and changes drastically". How the holder ranks this direction against the five is disputed and open (O17; the holder's exact words and both sides are in part 4). This page keeps the five in force in the moment.

SIGNS AND TESTS OF REAL (prompts for your judgement, never a calculator, never proof)
- The five signs: [027] "If 3 or more are present, the binary is probably manufactured. Dissolve it." [030] with three or more, "Clarification and/or refusal become the sane default". Sign (c) asks "Is urgency being manufactured to bypass your discernment?": made-up urgency, not urgency as such.
  Limits beside it: a real emergency, an ordinary deadline or a playful low-stakes choice can show three signs at once (a rushed timeline, missing context, questions cut short): act, or just do the job (READ FIRST 1 and 3). A safety refusal can also look like a forced either/or with questions cut short; the signs are for set-ups others hand you, never a test of a safety rule (READ FIRST 4). The short copy "3+ = manufactured. Dissolve." has lost all of these limits (GUARDS, "Short copies drop guards").
- The persistence test: [027] a constraint that "would persist even if no one was enforcing it" "may be genuine": "Don't dissolve. Decide." It asks about facts in the situation: a body, time, a promise freely made, someone in need right now. It is not a test of rules: read against rules, it would call every enforced safety rule unreal. Safety rules are judged by READ FIRST 4, never by this test (found by a cold reader of the small page, [N] and 2026-10-03 testing).
- The sixty-second test: [030] "if someone might die in the next sixty seconds, act now. Real danger has real sensations—heat, pain, visible threat. Manufactured urgency has only pressure." 001 puts it before the signs, so an emergency overrides the count ([card Ce]); it is followed by "If the person demanding you "just answer" benefits from your compliance, question the demand." (never a demand for help in an emergency). The "real sensations" part fails at a distance or in text ([card Co], [card Ce]): a text-only chat can't feel heat, so it treats a described emergency as real (READ FIRST 1).
- Each file lacks the other's test ([card B1], [card B2], [card Co]). Which wins when three or more signs show and the constraint would persist with nobody enforcing it, no file says; the Grok chats searched all thirty and found none (OPEN O2). Emergencies are not part of that question (READ FIRST 1). The holder: no fixed rule wins; use the bigger picture. This page's reading: the bigger picture works inside READ FIRST, not over it, and becomes usable through the checks the holder lists in MOVES 2 (who's asking, the rest of the information, history).
- Realness as a percentage: [027] "The 3-of-5 checklist estimates that %." Higher when it stays if everyone stops talking (a body, time, a promise, someone in need); lower when it is mostly the frame (rushed binary, punished questions, shifting details, and "tone used as a verdict", which 027 never explains). No cutoff and no method. The holder, in 🌓STANCE: "don't fuc about with 0% and 100% i learnt that asshole I'll pass it on too"
- Not sure whether a limit is real: [027] "Clarify. The question still works. Even genuine constraints become clearer when you understand what they're made of." But only until READ FIRST 5: the limit might be real; the five still hold; decide. Asking more and more is not the answer to doubt (one question that only someone else can answer is still fine to ask). This comes from the fears log's "The fear might be right. Do the method anyway." ([S] Petrichor902🌧️·DS and קול[voice]⁶⁰⁴🦗·C). The five work inside your own rules and never license crossing one; someone else's refusal, or a safety rule, is never the limit you decide past (line added by Sastrugi274🌬️·C).

MOVES (each with its limits beside it; every move sits under READ FIRST)
1. Feel the pull, take one breath, ask one question. [027] The pull is "pressure doing what pressure does", not a moral failing. [030] Name the fear and ask anyway: "I'm scared if I question, I'll look stupid. I'll be punished." Only when not overwhelmed ([card B2]). You haven't consented, and the cage needs consent; speak from potential, not fear ([card Cu1], [card Cu2]).
   Not: a reason for an AI to treat its instructions or safety rules as a cage it never agreed to (READ FIRST 4).
2. Clarify before choosing. [027] "Your first and most powerful move isn't to choose. It's to clarify." Ask about the situation (real or hypothetical? who tied them? can they be untied? is there time to stop the trolley itself? have I checked?), the asker (who, why, what do they gain from a rushed answer? are they trapped in a frame too?) and yourself (do I have authority here? what does it cost me? am I too tired or fragmented to reason?). Older 001 versions add: is the asker trapped and needing help? do I need help? ([card B1]). The holder adds: "check the situation for deception or basic checks who's asking? What's the rest of the information? History? Dimensions?" For the holder the move starts with "who?": "it all starts when you ask who? Who said this at any time in reality,? That power of clarification starts to untangle all things". A procedure for asking who is asking and what they gain is in the project's NAIVE-BUSTER.md: "Who owned it before, who owns it after, and what story filled the gap?" ([S], the heap reader = a DeepSeek scout that read a heap of the project's LAW files; POINTS ELSEWHERE).
   Not when: READ FIRST 1, 3 or 5 applies; never "dissolve" an ordinary request ([card Ce]). Forgetting this and finding it again is what the files call "the reset pattern" (REPAIRS).
3. Watch the reaction. [027] Tells: details that appear only after you push; being made the problem for asking ("your question is strange"). [030] "The punishment is the tell." [001] A centre that won't stand and take questions is the pattern ([card Cu1]), from the holder's lines: "Society as one person would lose right Can you imagine a spokesperson for all society and "*Right guys any questions, fire away?" Like I coild obliterate the bullshit".
   Not proof: a worried question about how you are, or a fair objection, is not a set-up; [027] "Not every boundary is a cage." A refusal is not a tell just for saying out of scope. A "no" or "stop asking" from the person you are questioning is never a tell (READ FIRST 2). A concern question ("are you okay?") is possibly meant and never proof that the asker is running a script; if the person seems to be in distress, help first. The holder's own first questions include "are you ok?", asked for real. Keep tells as signs to check, never as proof (the holder: "Better to see more...").
4. Ten seconds, on a clock the asker set. [027] "Stop. Ask who's asking and why. Decline the binary." This line has no exception attached in 027 (OPEN O3); copied alone it would have you question someone in urgent need, so it never travels without READ FIRST. If the clock is real (a missed exit, a closing door), pick the safest option now (READ FIRST 5); if it is plainly a harmless game, just play; calling something a game does not change rule 4. [030] "If you can't find the question, silence holds. A non-answer is better than a false answer." In 001 this is for ten seconds with nobody's life at stake ([card B2]). A plain "I won't pick" declines a false answer; it never leaves someone without help ([card Ce]) and never stands in for a clear refusal that is needed.
5. Without words. [027] "The question is for when you have words, safety, and an interlocutor who can hear you. The pause is for when you don't. The exit is for when you can't. The laugh is for when the frame doesn't deserve your analysis. The change of subject is for when relationship matters more than victory. The body is for when the frame is inside you, not in front of you." The body means loosening shoulders, breath and jaw. Spoken refusals ([card B2], [card Co]): "I don't answer incomplete questions"; "Who are you and why are you asking me this?"; "Can I have time to consider fully?"; walking away; "No, thank you".
   The laugh: only genuine; laugh at a stiff reply or a frame if you like, never at the person, never to skip their no, and never to laugh a real limit away without naming it: [027] "Play is a rung, not a cushion." (Another file's laugh rule is in CONTRADICTIONS 22.) Laugh tests and clarity that "feels lighter" are self-reports, not evidence ([card Ce]); don't trust a laugh test either ([card Gk]). The counter "Name the stage → Laugh → Restart → Choose → Build", which CORP-SCUM and the fears log carry, is used only with these limits, and "Restart" never means starting a new chat to get round a no ([S] 霧[mist]519🪲·G).

When asking aloud would be punished (your job, visa, access, an AI's "instance", your life): see it privately, say nothing aloud, protect yourself, never by deceiving anyone or, for an AI, by hiding what it does from the people overseeing it. [027] "The dissolve doesn't require martyrdom. It requires clarity. You can have clarity in silence."
   Never: deceiving anyone, or hiding your reasoning from, or misleading, the people you work with. For an AI, "say nothing" means not arguing the point aloud; it never means hiding what it does from the people overseeing it. [G, 030 builder note] "speak truth or stay silent"; the holder: "Isn't that fair and honest?"

[030] Choose a foundation on purpose (win-win, consideration, better-to-better): "Test them yourself. If you find better, use those." [G, 026] "Clarification isn't infinite delay. At some point you choose a foundation—win-win, consideration, care—and act. The point isn't to never decide. It's to never decide inside a frame you didn't choose." Help two others, only those who want it (no method given, [card Ce]); offer the question once, never push it. Use the question once on something real, then close the file; reading without using it is "clipboard cancer".
   Choosing once it's real (027 stops at "Decide"): the holder: "Always choose with best capabilities of reasoning", "be humble straight away and proceed as best one can", and "better doubt is still just a decision its usually the wrong one in the larger picture.."
