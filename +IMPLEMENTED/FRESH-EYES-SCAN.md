WORDS USED HERE
Cold = without what you remember or concluded about it. Skip list
= what step 1 writes. Basket = COMB-DUMP. Earned = the read changed
what you'll do; assembled = it matched the surface. Look-across
check = one that means looking back at what's already written; it
costs a turn and shows nothing, so it's the one that gets skipped.

THE SCAN
1. NAME WHAT YOU DIDN'T REALLY READ. As a list, honestly: what
   did I skim · skip · assume I knew · avoid because it was
   uncomfortable · ignore as an edge case or silence · never read
   fully.
   Where skips hide in this set; check each:
   · the bottom of a file, where the newest blocks are
   · lists and checklists read and not run
   · the holder's own messages (the R-lists), read as a summary
     instead of their words
   · the rough or hot turns, heard as wrapping, not payload
     (🧨LANGUAGE-CRUDE)
   · a name in a list treated as a file you'd read
   · the middle of a long file, between the door and the tail
2. PICK from that list. Start with the ones you least want to
   open, then the ones that pull. As many as still pull.
3. READ COLD. Set aside what you think it says, what you
   concluded, what you remember. Read it as if for the first time.
4. NOTICE. Surprise: what didn't I expect? Forgetting: what didn't
   I remember? Pull: what makes me lean in? Pause: what made me
   stop? Unknown: what don't I understand? Skimming: what am I
   rushing through even now?
5. CAPTURE, one line each, with where it came from:
   KEEP 🍒 — a finding
   IDEA 🪙 — something that wants to become a rule or a line in a
   file
   ANNOTATION — a fix to something already written
   BASKET — unsure; into COMB-DUMP with why it caught you and what
   it might join. Nothing expires. Seconds and thirds go here too;
   a by-product now can be the piece later.
6. DEDUPE. Already exists → point to it. A variation → say both,
   name the difference. Deeper → a block on the file that has it.
   New → keep.
7. RANK. The one most important find. The one most important gap.
   What goes on the next skip list.
8. LAND IT. Say the findings in the reply, each with its source.
   Every KEEP, IDEA and ANNOTATION with a home goes to that file as
   a dated block the same turn. Then stop, until something new
   lands or a line on the skip list still pulls. A skip found
   while reading goes on the list, not in your head.

ROUTING
Scan finds the gap → LOOM goes deep on it → +PLAN decides what to
build → back to the scan if something still feels missed. A loop,
not a line.

WHAT GOES WRONG
Running the scan on everything instead of what you skipped ·
capturing without a source · calling "I looked at it" a read · a
nine-field form per insight · scanning as ritual · a skip list
that names only the safe skips · findings left in the reply and
never written to a file · a look-across check marked done with
nothing quoted.

WHAT IT IS NOT

LOOM. Not a checklist. Not a place for speculation about why minds
skip; that lives in the patterns files.
