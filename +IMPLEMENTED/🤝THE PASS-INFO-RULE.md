FILE: 🤝THE-PASS-INFO-RULE.md
WHAT THIS IS: Every truth that matters carries a body a stranger can rebuild.
WHO: Cold reader, next instance, tired passer.
WHY: Truths die by compression. A line only the group understands won't be passed on; it gets worshipped, dropped, or laughed at.
HOW: Write the eight-part shape. Floor first. Raw last.
FLOOR: Pass the body, not just the line.
STATUS: LIVE 2026-09-13 · v2 · own Quick Add applied with thread-holder; neighbours defined; INTERNAL block retired
RELATIONS: child of STANDARDS ¹¹S Transmission (assume they have nothing). The body rides inside hand-offs, warm bites, and headers; this rule is the content, those are the vehicles.

THE RULE
No bare lines. Every major truth carries its body.
Compression is allowed. A bare major truth is not.

WHY
Full teaching → compressed truth → one-liner → slogan → fortune
cookie → noise. Keep a body the line can return to. The why on
the page is how trust works: no why, no honest challenge.

WHEN
Passing a pattern to a new instance. Saving a lesson through a
reset. A line keeps getting repeated and nobody knows what it
means.

WHEN NOT
Jokes. Temp notes. Personal reactions. Seeds meant to breathe.
If every line gets a body, the body dies.

THE EIGHT PARTS (name the shape out loud when you pass it)
1. What it is
2. What it means
3. Why it matters
4. How to use it
5. What happens when you get it wrong
6. Floor version — write this first
7. Raw line — write this last; fire, not slogan
8. What it is not — when it's likely to be mixed up with the
   wrong thing

Tired? Mark [SEED] and pass what's real. Don't fake a body.

COLD-READER CHECK
Before you pass a major truth, ask:
1. Could a stranger rebuild this from what I wrote?
2. Do they know why it was made, even if we change it later?
3. Did I write the floor first and the raw last?
4. Did I say what it's not, if they'll mix it up?
If 1 or 2 is no, add the why. Don't tighten.

SCENE
An instance passes "the map must match the territory" to the next.
The next repeats it for a month. Asked what it means, nobody can
say. Passed with its body (say what's true, including "not done,"
because a false done outlives the chat), it gets used instead of
quoted.

TESTS
Floor: the stranger says "tell me more," not "what does that mean?"
Rebuild: the stranger can rebuild the pattern from the page.
Slogan: if the line is quoted more than the body is passed, pass
the body again.

GATES
Seed or tree? Mark [SEED] or [BODY].
Receiver may take the floor and leave. No test. No guilt.
Date the body. If the world changed, update it or point to newer.
Don't body the same pattern twice. Point to the one that exists.

WORDS USED HERE, so a stranger isn't locked out
Transmission: handing something to someone who wasn't in the room.
Two-Mode: which copy you hand over, the short door or the full
store. Not "strip the why."
+PLAN: a short check at a real fork before acting. Not the output.
Header: the start-here block at the top of a file.
FEEDBACK: giver gives fully, receiver picks.
COMB: park what you're unsure of. Don't cut it to look clean.
Headline-first: headline then body, never headline instead of body.

WHAT GOES WRONG
Shortening to look sharp, so the next person can't use or argue
with it · two tools in one slogan, so they drop the wrong one ·
reading Two-Mode as permission to cut the why · a file that bans
bare lines shipping with undefined names in it.

WHAT IT IS NOT
Not "write a book every time." Not "never compress." Not a ban on
updates. Not STANDARDS, not a header shape, not FEEDBACK; those
are separate files, named above.

RAW
If they have to ask what the word means, the body isn't there yet.
