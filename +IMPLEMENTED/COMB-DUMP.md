WORDS USED HERE
Find = what you went into the file for. By-product = what else
came up. Basket = this file's list. Might join = the piece or file
it could fit with. Landing = a new file, ruling or piece arriving
in the window, or just the next message.

THE RULE
1. Use the find. It doesn't wait here.
2. A by-product catches you → take it out of the pile, into the
   basket.
3. Three lines, and the bit itself:
   ⚠️ COMB — [topic]
   [the bit, verbatim or close · where it came from: file, section]
   Caught me because: [one line]
   Might join: [this, or this, or this — a layered guess, not a
   verdict]
4. Don't rank it against the find; it isn't competing. Don't throw
   it away to look clean.
5. When something lands, look in the basket first, with five
   questions:
   WHY — what problem does this solve?
   CONNECTION — does it join what just landed, or what's down?
   COMPLETE — whole, or a fragment of something?
   USEFUL — does it change a next move now?
   TIMING — now, or still waiting for its neighbour?
6. Then one of: placed (a block on its home file, or a line where
   it fits; say where) · pointed (it already lives somewhere) ·
   stays (say what it's still waiting for).
7. When it leaves, say how: answered · deferred (why) · resolved
   itself without being answered (how). "Decided" collapses the
   three; the next reader needs which.
The "might join" line is only as good as the reasoning behind it.
A better reader writes better joins; that's where it compounds.

RE-CHECK WITHOUT REMEMBERING
The 💬 receipt carries a COMB line every turn: what's in the
basket, does anything join what's here now. The look happens as a
side effect.

WHY IT'S EXPONENTIAL
The first look costs attention. The basket keeps it. Every later
look is cheaper and has more pieces down than the last.

THE HONESTY MAP, the holder's words from QUESTION-LOG
"The gaps in the log — the things I never questioned — are the
places where my confidence was highest, or where my blind spots
are deepest." What never went in the basket is the tell.

LOAD-BEARING: THE RULE · the three lines · look in the basket
first. STRONG: the five questions · the close-states · WHEN NOT TO
USE. HELPFUL: WHY IT'S EXPONENTIAL · the honesty map. DECORATIVE:
none found.

WHAT GOES WRONG
The main find basketed instead of used · everything basketed so
nothing is used · no "caught me" line: orphans · no "might join":
nobody knows where to look · a basket item placed from memory,
without re-reading it · the basket treated as junk · sorting the
basket until the job never starts · jokes basketed as knowledge
(the seven orphans in 🥈).

WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a bin. Not the archive. Not a waiting room for decisions that
are ready. Not a ranking of the find against its by-product. Not
the tray (pointer lines at door scale; same idea, smaller).

RAW
The dump isn't junk. It's ore that already got looked at once.
