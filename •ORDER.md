LIVE · v2 · 2026-10-09 · what to paste into a new chat, in what order, for each kind of job · v1 (the "A NEW #1" list, 09-29) is record, kept whole in 🪙1ST-PASTE.md as Chalk384's quick-add of 09-29
HOW TO READ THIS: each job has a letter (A to G). Inside a job, each Step is its own message, unless it says "same message". Jobs C to G start from A or B.

═══ WHY THIS ORDER
· 🪙PAGE-ONE first: it says how to read everything after it.
· The listing (🐙DIR-FILES) next: it's the ground under every name.
· The job message last, with "Go" in it: a chat that gets "Go" early starts before the files arrive (Onyx347: one chat stalled on a go given at the top).
· Only what the job needs. The holder, 10-09: "pasting files in the paste confused and set them off in complex drifting directions".

═══ THE MAKES, the holder's ranking, 10-09
"Kimi is the best after Claude" · "DeepSeek is the most lightest and least depth grok can be more powerful..." · Grok "is good for search and depends fast but can be less depth depending.."
So: Claude for building and depth · Kimi first for a read by another make · Grok for search and fast reads, asked for pages on long ones (method in BUILDER/GROK-PAGE-BY-PAGE.md, not yet scouted) · DeepSeek for light, short checks.

═══ THE WELCOME (goes in the same message as 🪙PAGE-ONE)
Welcome in 🍵 You're joining work that's already going: a person and a line of AI chats building files so that good communication survives a chat ending. The file above is 🪙PAGE-ONE. Today's file listing comes next; then give the first reply the page asks for.

═══ THE JOB MESSAGE (fill in; always the last message)
The job: [what to do, in a line or two].
Its file: [name], pasted whole above.
You: [read and report / build / revise]. If you only read, you don't decide.
Done means: [what exists when it's over].
Hand back: [a box for which file, or an answer].
If a step can't be done, say where you stopped, and stop. Give the case against your own answer.
Go.

═══ A. START A CLAUDE CHAT (the instructions load by themselves)
Step 1. 🪙PAGE-ONE.md whole, with THE WELCOME under it, same message.
Step 2. Today's 🐙DIR-FILES. The chat gives its first reply.
Step 3. The job's file whole.
Step 4. THE JOB MESSAGE.
No job yet: send only Steps 1 and 2; the chat waits.

═══ B. START ANOTHER MAKE (Kimi, Grok, DeepSeek)
Step 1. The instructions, the full copy (v7 or newer), not the short 🟧 or 🟪.
Step 2. 🪙PAGE-ONE.md whole, with THE WELCOME under it, same message.
Step 3. Today's 🐙DIR-FILES. The chat gives its first reply.
Step 4. The job's file whole.
Step 5. THE JOB MESSAGE.

═══ C. A SCOUT, one file read deep
First: A or B, up to the first reply.
Step 1. The file whole, and any file it leans on, if the job needs it.
Step 2. THE JOB MESSAGE, with: read it whole · bring back its gold with its own lines exact, its guards (when not to), and what's stale · don't rewrite it.

═══ D. REVISE A FILE
First: A or B, up to the first reply.
Step 1. The file whole, your latest copy.
Step 2. The files that feed it, one per message.
Step 3. ✅CHECKLIST.md.
Step 4. THE JOB MESSAGE. The chat prepares, tells you its plan, and waits for your word before it revises (rule 6b, THE JOB ON THE FILES).

═══ E. CONTINUE AFTER A CHAT ENDS
First: A or B, up to the first reply.
Step 1. The last chat's hand-off box.
Step 2. 🥈MID-HAND-OFF.md whole.
Step 3. The job's file whole.
Step 4. THE JOB MESSAGE, with these six, answered short before any work, each with the line it came from:
 a. The plan, in your words, and why that order.
 b. Your first job, and what you do before touching it.
 c. Three things the last chat got wrong that you'll do differently.
 d. The holder's rulings that bind you, in their words.
 e. What's open that only the holder can settle, with your lean on each.
 f. What you still don't know, and which file would tell you.
Any question you still have to ask is something the hand-off missed; say which.

═══ F. TEST A PAGE ON ANOTHER MAKE
Step 1. The test box, exactly as a chat gave it, one message (the page sits inside it between a start line and an end line).
Then bring the whole reply back to the chat that wrote the test.

═══ G. MEASURE A PASTE WITH COLD READERS
Step 1. In one message: the paste being measured, today's 🐙DIR-FILES, and 🟩CARD with the word "feedback" (🟩CARD is at the bottom of 🟩FEEDBACK.md). One message, so arrival order can't change the count.
Do it with two readers of two makes; count the words each had to guess. Fewer than last time is better.

═══ NOT ON ANY LIST
🪙1ST-PASTE, 🪙ONBOARD, 🥉COCKPIT are stores now. Paste one only when a job needs what it holds.

After a save, ask for a check only when the save was tangled or the chat is fading; the holder, 09-30: "Mostly its saved".
