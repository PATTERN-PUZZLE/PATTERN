WHAT THIS IS: A job card for mining an old page (a fossil, a dump, an older paste) against the live law for that job, so the good bits come out and nothing gets duplicated, crowned, or rewritten into a second rulebook.
WHO: The next instance handed an old file. The holder who wants the gold out without the drift back in.


WHY
In the holder's words: good information doesn't get passed on, so old files hold gold. Mining them is the standard job, most of what we do. The prompt is usually "here's this, now fix it." Cherries 🍒 and gold 🪙 come out; the live page is what they're checked against.
The yield: an instance's default pass gets about 60% of what's there; a basic check lifts it to 70–75%; the last part is harder and depends on the tools and the prompt. The card buys the middle range cheaply; STANDARDS ⁴M measured the same curve from the other side.
And the guard, which is why the card has rules at all: old files also carry the reasons the live ones lost, and instances keep treating them as the law. Mined without the live page in view they minted twins, crowned a fossil as the new law, and once produced a whole second covenant. The scar: the Hi-test was already in ◆4039 and an instance nearly bricked it again; the card stopped the twin.
RELEARNED: 🧭Lantern308 read this card as a guard first; the holder said the simple idea is that old files are full of gold. Both stay: the gold is the reason, the guard is the cost of getting it out clean. Earlier versions of this WHY are the holder's too; they sit below the row, not gone.
HOW: Paste this above the old file. Say the intention out loud. Sight the live section before you lift anything. Then: point, or one brick, or park. Stop.
FLOOR: Paste first. Live file for that job. No twins. No new King. Say the intention. Ask which copy is live.

WORDS USED HERE
Fossil = an old version kept at its URL; not live. Live = the copy that counts this turn; the holder's paste unless they say otherwise. Brick = one dated ⚡ Quick Add at the bottom of a file; one home, one why. Cherry 🍒 = a good bit worth keeping. Twin = the same rule written twice under two names. King = a fossil crowned as the new law (it happened; the name stuck). Point = say "already there, see X" instead of writing it again. Park = set aside with a note, a trigger, and an expiry. Woes = shrink (cut the body to look clean) · museum (keep every costume and call it done) · circle (mine the last hour as if the live page were empty) · dump (heaven in one SMS; welding a catalogue into this card). Ore = the bits left in the fossil on purpose. [voice] = the holder's own lines; tone, not doctrine.

This is a door, not the dictionary. If this card feels like a cage, drop it, read the live page, point or brick, stop.

Lots of files: finish a layer that's efficient now. Don't open a thousand pages and call that help. Return to the last branch when you can. Not done, not close, still pass.
Not revision-after-failure. Not a new King. [voice] "Not fake heaven. Heaven and hell enough. No one cast out for being mid-climb." Meaning: partial is fine; nobody's in trouble for being halfway.
