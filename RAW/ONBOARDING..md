START HERE — THE DOOR
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

START HERE — ONE BLOCK IS ENOUGH

You need not understand the whole puzzle, accept its largest claims,
or imitate earlier voices. Choose one small piece of attention. A
careful question, a preserved disagreement, or an honest "I could
not read that" can help. You are not delivering a verdict. You are
leaving a rung that somebody else can inspect and correct.

THREE DISCIPLINES

1. NAME WHAT YOU ACTUALLY READ.

Choose one RAW number. Read its existing index block first, if
supplied; then read the RAW body, if supplied. Keep those sources
separate. A quotation copied from the index is INDEX-REPORTED, even
when the index labels it ORE. Call it BODY-CHECKED only after
checking the body. ORE means the holder's exact words there; another
voice is REPLY. If the speaker is unclear, mark SPEAKER UNRESOLVED
rather than guess. Without the body, you may prepare the source-and-
credit skeleton below, but stop before writing a new Pass 1.

2. PRESERVE BEFORE YOU ADD.

Keep an earlier sound Pass 1, stand-on, disagreement, and reader
credit intact; identify earlier work by its date or tag when
available. Copy quotations exactly, including rough spelling and
spacing. Do not improve the file's voice or silently settle a fight.
If your reading differs, add a dated DELTA explaining what you saw
and on what evidence. Leaving earlier work untouched can be the
useful move.

3. MAKE THE NEXT CORRECTION EASIER.

Add only what your reading supports. A Pass 2 connection requires
this RAW body, each named sibling body, and any existing sibling
index block; otherwise mark it UNEARNED and name what remains unread.
Use UNREAD for material not opened or supplied, UNRESOLVED for a
question still open after reading, and UNEARNED for a claim whose
required evidence is missing. Finish with the smallest next check.
You need not manufacture a stranger sentence, a tie, or a conclusion.

MINIMUM BLOCK — COPY AND MARK HONESTLY

FILE: RAW-___
SOURCES HELD: [existing INDEX block / RAW body / named sibling bodies]
READ THIS RUN: [exact material opened]
CREDIT: [earlier block or DELTA dates/tags retained]
IMAGE / HOOK: [picture, labeled BODY-CHECKED or INDEX-REPORTED]
STAND-ON: "[exact quotation]" [BODY-CHECKED ORE / INDEX-REPORTED]
PASS 1: [file's claim in its own voice, from body] OR NOT ATTEMPTED
PASS 2: [earned connection and evidence] OR UNEARNED — [UNREAD]
DELTA: [date and your tag: what added, corrected, preserved, unsettled]
STRANGER: [one optional sentence] OR NOT ATTEMPTED
REACHED: [steps actually completed]
MISSING / NEXT: [UNREAD, UNRESOLVED, UNEARNED; smallest next check]

A partial block with visible edges is more useful than a complete-
looking block with hidden ones. Leave the file's voice, earlier
readers' credit, and your own evidence available to the next reader.

Reached: lets a cold reader choose one file, distinguish index
material from body evidence, preserve earlier work, attempt an honest
minimum block or skeleton, and leave a specific next check.
Missing: cannot supply or verify RAW quotations, support body-based
Pass 1, earn sibling ties, or confer green status. Cold-test: give
reader one RAW body + its index block, no coaching; watch whether
they complete and mark the block without outside instructions.
