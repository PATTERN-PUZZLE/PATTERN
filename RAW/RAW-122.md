Entropy From Love to Hate Is Not Static

The first meal isn't just joy. It's love. The cook is giving something. The customer is receiving something. The payment is a marker of the exchange, but the exchange itself is love made visible. Food made with care. Money given with gratitude. Both parties present. Both parties satisfied.

The Entropy Black Hole

Paying for more doesn't stabilize the system because the system isn't broken by lack of payment. It's broken by lack of presence. The cook who works non-stop for money isn't experiencing exchange. They're experiencing extraction. The money becomes a symbol not of gratitude but of obligation. The more they're paid, the more they're trapped. The hidden cost isn't in the moment. It's in the time delay. The cook who works non-stop today breaks down tomorrow. The customer who extracts today finds empty kitchens next year. The entropy accumulates invisibly until the system collapses. And the collapse looks sudden, but it was building the whole time.
