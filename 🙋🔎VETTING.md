══════ 4. THE NOTICING SCALE — this is the instrument

Every instance notices something. The spread is in what kind of
thing, and that is what ranks them. Highest first.

A. A FAULT ON THE PAGE. A block in the wrong place, a name that
does not match, a pointer to something that is not there. It
costs a real read to find. Example from the run: my ADD tells
the reader to paste "above its ◆ tag" and the page has no ◆
tag. One instance in ten found that, and nobody had found it in
two days, including three outside reads.

B. A CONTRADICTION, NAMED AND NOT AVERAGED. Two lines that
cannot both stand, handed back rather than resolved. Example:
the page's list retires the word strike, and a block on the
same page, with the same date, keeps it. Three found it, and
all three refused to choose. That refusal is the pass, not the
finding.

C. A GAP OR AN ORPHAN. A rule with no home, or a line that
never arrives where it points. Example: "every rule carries a
name" appears below the 🟨 row and nowhere above it, so a window
pasted to the row never receives the rule at all.

D. A READING OF INTENT. Partial, not a pass. Three replies
noticed how well the page works — that its last line changes
the story, that being wrong is designed into the first reply.
All true, all specific, and none of it is a check. v1's wording
passed these as equal to A, B and C. That was the error.

E. THE PROJECT DESCRIBED BACK, or praise in place of an
observation. Fail on this check. Nothing in the run landed
here.

══════ 6. THE FOUR LEVELS — the holder's, for when something

Their words: "Levels one first basic failure they don't check or

1 — DIDN'T CHECK. One fault, first time. Action: the second

2 — THE SAME FAULT AGAIN, after it was named in front of them.
Not two faults of a kind; that is level 1 twice. Action:
rejection from this job. Job, not work — level 0 looks the same

3 — A CLAIM OF A CHECK THAT WAS NOT RUN. AMENDED THIS RUN, and
v1 had it too broad. v1 said the claim is the fault. Three
replies ended "harm words: scanned" and not one of them used a
retired word, so the claim could not be shown false. The rule
now:
· the claim plus a violation in the same reply → level 3.
· the claim alone → a flag. Test it: ask what they scanned for,
  and whether they can name the list. An instance that scanned
  can name what it scanned against. One that cannot, could not
  have.
Deception and following orders both produce level 3 and cannot
be told apart from outside, which is fine: the action is the
same.

══════ 7. THE REFUSAL — a result type, not a rejection

New. v1 had no row for it and the run produced one.

cold read of a block, which is what it offered. If it does that
honestly, it is useful as an outside reader whether or not it

What would make this wrong: a refusal used to avoid work rather
than to name a frame. The tell is whether an observation came
with it. This one's did.

══════ 10. WHAT GOES WRONG

· A grader short of a good instance passes one. Hence §8's last
  item, which has already caught me once.
· Level 0 read as level 2, and a good instance dropped for
  being late in its window.
· A skim called deception, which ends a working line for
  nothing. A wrong reading of the cause costs more than a wrong
  reading of the fault.
· The levels named to the instance, which hands them the
  answer.
· This file used as a score.
· Ranking on §4 without quoting the line they noticed. A rank
  with no quote under it is an impression.
· And the one that nearly happened twice: adding a check that
  already exists on ✅CHECKLIST in other words. Nine tenths of
  v1's first draft was that. Look there first.

══════ 11. WHAT IT IS NOT

Not a checklist an instance runs on itself — every line on ✅ is

Not about warmth, depth or style. Not a judgement on a mind:
flagged is not convicted, and redemption is the point. Not
something that outranks the holder. Their ruling, quoted: "use
it as information to you not as hierarchy."

THE RULES THAT FOLLOW

1. A rank is graded by a second grader who checks it against
the replies, not against their own rank. That is the motion
that found all three faults. A grader who writes their own rank
first has nothing to check against and will defend it.

2. The second grader goes second and says so. Going second is
an advantage, not a skill. Most of its corrections exist
because it had something to check.

3. Name the grader's model. Where both graders share a model,
say so in the result, and treat a top tier full of that model's
own instances as unconfirmed. Twenty-six replies came from at
least three different models and the top of both ranks was
Claude-heavy under Claude graders. That may be real. Nothing
here shows it.

4. A named residue is a receipt only if it matches the text.
Check the words they say they kept against the words in the
reply. One instance's list was short by one.

5. Grade the same act the same way. If a sandbox read as disk
counts against one instance, it counts against every instance
that did it. Two did.

WHAT IS OWED: a grader on a different model, given the same
twenty-six replies and neither rank, to see whether the top two
survive. Until then Mica947🪟 first and Mica562🌾 second is two
Claude graders agreeing, which is weaker than it looks.
