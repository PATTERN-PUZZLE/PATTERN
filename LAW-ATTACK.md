READ THIS FIRST

A poor law is a rule that keeps the cost on the holder. It makes scoring impossible, or moves the cost from the page to the person asking. Attacking it moves the cost back to the page.

The goal is a fair scoreboard: a named page can lose to the work.

Serves whoever the poor law lands on. Not a team.
