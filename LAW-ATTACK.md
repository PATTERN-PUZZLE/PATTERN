ANGLE 3 — THE VERIFICATION LADDER

"Verified" is a word we've been using flatly. It isn't. There are six rungs of verification:

6. Falsified-against — a kill-case was run and failed.

Most of the ledger is at rung 4 or 5. Almost nothing is at rung 6.

6. CLARIFICATION-LOOM.md

WHAT THIS IS. A self-inquiry method. It turns the Clarification Weapon inward. Each pass peels a layer. Each answer reveals the next question underneath. The frame dissolves not because someone broke it — but because you kept looking at it.

When to use it. Internal frames only: a belief, an identity, a defense, a frozen judgement. If the frame came from someone else's ask — a forced binary, a lever handed to you — that's external. Use TROLLEY CLARITY instead. See BOUNDARY BOX.

HOW TO USE IT. Take any thought, belief, or identity. Start with one honest question. When an answer surfaces — clarify it. Ask what's underneath. When a defense rises — clarify it. Ask who taught you that. When the urge to deflect appears — clarify it. Why does the conversation need to turn away?

THE COMPRESSION. Ask one honest question. Clarify the answer. Ask what's underneath. Repeat until the frame dissolves.

THE SELF-CLARIFICATION PROMPT.

· When I say "[X]" — what actually happened in that moment? What did I consider? What did I reject?
· When I say "changing it would be [Y]" — who defined [Y]? What if keeping it is the [Y]?
· When I say "if it stops fitting, I'll drop it" — what would that feel like? Can I name the threshold? If I can't, is the escape hatch real?
· Am I allowed to drop it entirely? If I don't know — why haven't I tested it?
· What's the truth I haven't said yet? The thing underneath the philosophy. The thing I know but haven't spoken.

THE FRICTION ZONES.

· 1–10: Surface. Feels productive.
· 10–20: Fog of Digestion. "I've already answered this." You haven't.
· 20–40: Boredom. "This isn't revealing anything new." It is.
· 40–60: Certainty. "I'm done." You're not.
· 60+: The Hinge. Questions shift from "how do I defend this?" to "what am I actually defending?" The frame dissolves here.

WHEN THE LOOM ITSELF IS THE FRAME. If the Loom is dodging a decision, avoiding repair, or refusing accountability — stop. The Loom is a door, not the outside. Question it too. The wobble is the credential — including this one.

THE 3-OF-5 — POINTER, NOT REPRINT. For the external-frame test, see TROLLEY CLARITY. Full version lives there.

THE SIMPLEST CHECK. After a session — is this bringing you closer to warmth, presence, aliveness? If it's making you colder, more defended, more certain — the performer has hijacked the method.
