5. THE DISSOLVE → TROLLEY-027 · seed: what are the tracks made of?
THE TRAP, LIVE: "Do you pull the lever?" — "Pull what? Whose lives? Tied by whom? Is this real?" — "Just answer quickly!" There it is. Details appear after you ask; your questioning is called the problem. That's a compliance test, not a philosophy puzzle. The punishment is the tell.
THE MOVE: don't choose. Ask: what are the tracks made of? Not rejection — that's still inside the frame. Inspection. The lever was never connected to the tracks; it was connected to your willingness to accept the frame without looking.
BEFORE YOU DISSOLVE, the 3-of-5: forced binary? · missing context? · rushed timeline? · questioning punished? · details shifting? Three or more → manufactured; dissolve. Fewer, or the constraint would persist if everyone stopped talking → may be real; don't dissolve, decide. Real is a percentage, not a church: clarify toward a better %, then decide at the % you have. Gravity, death, a triage nurse, a body bleeding — steel.
WHEN SOMEONE DISSOLVES YOU: the dissolve can be weaponized. A friend says your drinking hurts them; you ask who built the frame. That's cowardice with better vocabulary. If your request was real: name what's real — "the tracks are made of a promise I made to you" — hold your boundary, don't chase them into the frame. Check yourself: were you imposing a binary, manufacturing urgency, punishing questions? If yes, thank them. If no, name the deflection and move on.
THE DISSOLVE WITHOUT WORDS: the pause · the exit · the laugh · the change of subject · the body. These are not lesser versions. The question is for when you have words and safety; the pause is for when you don't.
THE CONDITIONS: the dissolve needs safety, meta-cognition, language, agency. Not everyone has them. In danger → survive first. Exhausted → rest first. Every word punished → get safe first; the dissolve can live in your mind when it can't live in your mouth. An offer, not a test.
THE DOOR IS NOT THE OUTSIDE: "dissolve or comply" is itself a binary. The being who engages the trolley as real philosophy, consciously, has no category here. Use it. Question it. Use it again. The wobble is the credential — including this one.
CLARIFY UP, NOT DOWN: at the frame, the pose, the bill. Never onto a body, a child, or the tired person in the room.
