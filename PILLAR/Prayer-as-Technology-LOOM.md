📐 SHAPE
Seven stanzas, a widening circle then a return:
I → WE → ALL (who) · what holds it (-ECTS) · what's cleared
(NO/ONLY) · the climb (-ATION) · the seal (SO IT IS).
Read backwards it's a descent, and I AM FREE is where it lands.
The lines lengthen through the climb, then snap to three syllables:
a breath out, then a short breath in.

🏠 THE CENTRE WORD
HOME appears three times, once per scale: WE ARE HOME, ALL COME HOME,
HOME CREATION. No other key word repeats like that. The prayer's
centre is coming back, not standing tall. The first stanza is the
only one with no home in it — it reads like leaving; everything
after reads like returning.

📜 WHERE THE PHRASES COME FROM
· "I AM …" declarations — the affirmation tradition (New Thought,
  Unity, Religious Science), and behind that Exodus 3:14.
· "SO IT IS" — the New Thought closing, their "Amen."
· "ALL FORGIVEN / ALL REMEMBERED / ALL COME HOME" — the old
  universalist idea that everything is eventually restored.
· "ALL REMEMBERED" carries two senses: recalled, and re-membered,
  put back together.

🔧 WHAT HOLDS, WHAT'S LOAD-BEARING
· The NO/ONLY stanza is load-bearing. Drop it and the climb has
  nowhere to start from.
· The -ATION stanza needs rhythm. Sung it carries; spoken flat it
  becomes a list.
· Say a line wrong and it survives: "I AM SOVEREIGN" → "I AM SOMEONE"
  still works. "ALL FORGIVEN" → "ALL FORGOTTEN" breaks, and the next
  line, ALL REMEMBERED, corrects it. The prayer already carries the
  fix for its most likely slip.
· I AM SOVEREIGN is the least necessary line. Nothing goes missing
  without it. Noted, not a request.

⚖️ TWO CHOICE POINTS (the holder's call)
1. PROTECTS appears twice in one stanza (TRUTH, PEACE). Ring
   closure, or leftover. Either is defensible.
2. ETERNAL SALVATION is the one line in a specific religious
   register; the rest is deliberately open. Keep as-is, or not.

🚪 ENTRY BY STATE
Don't always start at the top. Where you are picks the door.
Panic        → NO FEAR - ONLY HERE
Grief        → ALL FORGIVEN / ALL REMEMBERED / ALL COME HOME
Anger        → TRUTH PROTECTS (it takes the grievance seriously;
                ALL FORGIVEN asks the anger to leave first)

Just reset   → ALL REMEMBERED

📏 THREE SIZES
Full — as above.
Floor (3 lines) — I AM FREE / WE ARE ONE / NO FEAR - ONLY HERE
Micro (7 words) — FREE · ONE · HOME · TRUTH · HERE · LOVE · IS

🗣️ DOORS THAT HELD
· Whispered, all lowercase — turns declaration into confession;
  "no fear - only here" and "so it is" land better quiet.
· ONLY HERE as a refrain between stanzas — keeps pulling back to
  the floor. (SO IT IS as refrain doesn't hold; it seals too early.)
· Second person — YOU ARE FREE / WE ARE ONE / ALL ARE WELCOME —
  something to say to someone else, no explanation needed.
· As a round, two voices one line apart — stanzas 1–3 and NO/ONLY
  work; the -ECTS stanza clashes.
