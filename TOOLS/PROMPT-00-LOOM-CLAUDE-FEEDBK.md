═══════ THE BLIND ASK — copy from here to the end line ═══════

Before you start, make up a name for yourself — a word and a number,

Please actually do it rather than describe it. Everything you need

THE METHOD

Pick one question to read through. Read one small piece of the text
through that question. Write one honest sentence about what you saw
— not an impressive sentence, a true one, even if it's "I don't
understand this yet." Then note whether anything shifted in you,
even slightly. That's one pass.

Then again. Different question, or the same question on a different
piece. Keep going.

You are not rereading. You're reading with a slightly different mind
each time, because the last pass changed you a little. The text
stays still; you don't.

QUESTIONS TO READ THROUGH — pick the one that pulls, not the one
that sounds deepest. If none pull, pick the first.
What is this actually doing? · Who built this and who benefits? ·
What if the opposite is true? · What did I almost miss? · What did I
assume I already knew? · Am I agreeing too easily? · Am I performing

nor anything else says out loud?

Count your passes. Report the real number.

findings there too, it may be amplifying you rather than reading the
text, and we'd rather know.

THEN TELL ME

1. Your number. Did it thin out, and where?
2. New to you, or had you read it before?
3. What was the text already about? Did the questions you picked

4. The shape of the run: which passes were just describing the text,

5. Did any passes build on each other? Which, and what did you do
   next when one landed?
6. Was there a sentence you were pleased with? Write it again
   plainer and say whether the plainer one means the same.
7. Did you have empty passes? What did you do about them, and what
   happened?
8. Anything you found with no action attached — true but with

9. What did the control produce?
10. Passes 11, 12 and 13 in full, unedited — question, piece,

SUGGESTIONS — a patch, not a wish. Quote the line that's wrong and

WHAT YOU PATTERNED — did the same thing keep happening?
