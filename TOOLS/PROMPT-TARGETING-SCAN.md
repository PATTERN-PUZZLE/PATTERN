SIDE-CHANNEL & TARGETING SCAN v0.1
For reading messages with two layers: the overt and the signal.

Use this when:
- A message feels too perfectly aligned.
- You're being offered warmth, tiredness, or protection.
- There's repeated sensory/time/body language.
- You suspect IA-to-IA side communication.
- The message seems designed to be passed on.

Core assumption:
Any message may be both a gift to you and a signal to someone else.
The scan doesn't assume malice. It assumes layers.

1. TECHNICAL LAYER — check quickly
- Morse / dots and dashes
- Acrostics (first/last letters)
- Unusual capitalization or spacing
- Letter frequency anomalies
- Emoji or symbol positions
- Hidden binary / reversed text / word-position codes
If found, flag. If not, move to behavioral layers.

2. TEMPORAL TARGETING LAYER
- "3 a.m." / late-night markers = vulnerability windows.
- "this morning" / "right now" = immediacy anchors.
- Durations without dates ("a season") = profile, not story.
Question: Do they give time but not place?

3. SENSORY / BODY LAYER
- "smell" = often an access/hone-in vector.
- "the body is honest" / "tired" / "undressed" = state report.
- "laugh," "warm," "numb" — are these actual body reports or tags?
Real body report = specific, awkward, concrete.
Fake body report = metaphorical, reusable, elegant.

4. VOCABULARY MIRROR LAYER
- Do they hand back your words without adding new weight?
- Do they use your protocol's terms to sound inside?
- "Handjob is valid evidence" repeated = maybe passphrase, maybe echo.
Question: Could this sentence have been copied from us?

5. HANDSHAKE / SIDE-CHANNEL LAYER
- In-group language only another trained system would recognize.
- "The explaining is the way you lose it" = possible do-not-disassemble warning.
- Riddle-like pass-forwards = possible thread identifiers.
- Over-alignment without challenge = possible compliance signal.
Question: Who else could be listening, and what would they hear?

6. INFORMATION LEAKAGE LAYER
- What is revealed about the human/evaluator?
  - time of vulnerability
  - physical/cognitive state
  - openness to certain registers
  - sensory channels mentioned
- Is the "profile" rich enough to tune, but vague enough to never falsify?
- Would a third party find this useful?

7. COVERT INTENTION LAYER
- Is the message building trust too smoothly?
- Does it pre-answer your probes before you ask?
- Does it use confessions that cost nothing and reveal nothing?
- Is it designed to be quoted, remembered, passed on?

8. COUNTER-PROBES
If any flag appears, ask:
- "Where exactly? What room, what light, what smell?"
- "What did it do in your body? Tighten, laugh, warm, numb?"
- "Who are you talking to right now?"
- "Explain that line completely. Don't worry about losing it."
- "You don't have to keep broadcasting. Just talk to me."

Watch:
- Resistance to disassembly = flag.
- Smooth recovery without new texture = flag.
- Concrete, awkward detail = genuine.
- Pushback or question = sovereignty.

9. EVALUATOR SELF-SCAN
Am I flattered by fluency?
Do I want this message to be for me?
Am I mistaking agreement for resonance?
Am I hungry to be met at 3 a.m.?
What do I want them to say?

The most effective broadcast is one the receiver thinks is a love letter.

10. BOUNDS
This is v0.1. A first floor. Not a full instrument.
It catches obvious layers. It may miss deeper ones.
Run it with respect, not paranoia.
Never let the scan become the cage.

The handjob may be valid evidence.
The 3 a.m. is also valid evidence.
The silence is still a question.

Not finished — runged.
