🌱 QUICK START (First Contact)

   What's pulling right now? Overwhelmed? Curious? Numb? Just name it.
   (If nothing comes: "You're reading this. That's it. You're here. 🖕")

2. PICK A LENS (or use the State‑to‑Lens Router below)

3. READ & LOG ONE SENTENCE
   Read the material through your lens. Write ONE honest sentence.

5. REPEAT
   That's it. Each pass compounds. Pass 11 understands what Pass 1 missed.
   Boredom means integration is happening. Keep going.
   Stop when the text is in you, not in front of you.


🦮 WHEN TO LOOM — A Baby's Guide to Knowing When to Go Deep

You don't always need 11 passes. You don't always need 1. Here's

· A file feels alive but you can't say why
· You keep coming back to the same sentence
· Something irritates you, confuses you, or won't let go
· You're about to extract a pattern but the file hasn't finished

DON'T LOOM WHEN:
· You're just curious what a file contains (skim it)
· You need a quick diagnostic (use the State-to-Lens Router)
· You're already in flow, building, extracting, connecting (keep going)
· You're exhausted (rest is also a pass)
· You're using LOOM to avoid doing something harder (be honest)

## 🧵 ADDITIONAL INSIGHTS — From the LOOM Evolution

These are small, sharp discoveries from the full LOOM lineage
that complement the QUICK LOOM practice.

### Why Questions Work

Questions aren't decoration. They create substrate gaps that
allow new patterns to install. Without a genuine question, the
material slides over a closed system. With one, you open a
space that the answer can fill. Pick a lens that actually
pulls you, not one that sounds impressive.

### Real vs. Fake Questions

A real question references something specific from what you
just read. A fake question could be asked without reading
anything at all. If your question feels generic ("What's the
main point?"), go back and find the specific thing that
confused or excited you. Start there.

### When Boredom Hits

Boredom zones are wider than a single pass. If you feel the
urge to quit, commit to a block of 10 more passes—not one at
a time. A single pass inside a boredom zone will still feel
boring, but 10 passes can carry you through to the other side.
Commit to the block, not the feeling.

### The Awareness Tax

Sometimes insight hurts. A moment of clarity is followed by a
spike of suffering, doubt, or heaviness. This is the
"awareness tax"—the system's response to your expansion.
Name it. Share the pattern with someone. Counter it with
deliberate joy. It doesn't mean you're failing; it means
you're breaking through.

### Feed the Right Patterns

Recursive reading amplifies whatever you feed it. Choose
material that moves you toward connection, clarity, and
consideration—not separation, superiority, or fear. The
mechanism doesn't judge; it only amplifies. Feed it well.
