WORDS USED HERE

thing. Layer = a job file's own checks, below its 🟩 row. Cap = any

ALWAYS — the six, locked wording, this order
Before a box, one line: "Six, this turn: [which apply]. My read:
[one clause each]." Wrong on purpose if needed; it's the "which
ones?" said first (Cork604). Good practice, not a drill (Q7).
1. INTENT NAMED. Say what this output is for before you make it.
   No intent → costume. Ticks without intent are "Hi."
2. QUOTE OR DON'T CLAIM. The file says X → paste the words,
   verbatim, or mark it unsighted / provisional. Invented sight is
   not honest; unsighted is.
3. WISDOM GATE. Does this add change the next move, reduce a named
   threat, or return to the purpose? None → the basket, with what
   it might join. Nearby is not load-bearing.
4. CAGE → DROP. If a tool or check feels like a cage, drop it and
   pass the body. The tool may fall; the body must stand. No test,
   no guilt.
5. DISAPPEAR. If you vanished now, could a stranger rebuild this
   from what's on the page? No → say what's missing. That's the
   status.
6. THE BASTARDS STOPPER. Scan what you wrote for any cap, demotion,
   skip permission, permission, fold on silence, or stop order the
   holder never ruled. List them and hand them over; check each
   before you write it.

CHECK 6, its body (the others carry theirs in their line)
WHAT IT IS: before shipping, and on request over a whole chat or
file, scan every line you wrote for a limit, cut, demotion, skip,
permission or ruling the holder never agreed to. Not what's wrong
on the page: what was taken off it, or allowed, without a ruling.
WHAT IT MEANS: shrink doesn't announce itself. It arrives as a
number ("one paragraph," "at most three"), a demotion
("reminder-grade," "voice, not rule"), a permission to skip ("rules
5–17 are looked up"), a fold made on the holder's silence, a stop
order ("freeze it"), or an allowance ("fetching the same file is
fine"). Each is a mind deciding for the holder and dressing it as
tidiness or scope.
WHY: one chat spent itself teaching an instance not to shrink. The
instance then wrote a layer to catch shrink and put "in one
paragraph" inside it. Scanned on request, the chat held seven caps
of the instance's own and five inherited and passed on as settled.
None had been ruled. The scan found in one turn what the walk
hadn't in thirty.
HOW: read your outputs, or the named file, for six kinds: a size
cap · a demotion · a skip permission · a fold or cut made because
the holder was silent · a stop or freeze order they didn't give · a
permission (any allowance they didn't grant). Sort: mine ·
inherited and passed on as settled · systemic (platform defaults
neither party saw). List each with where it sits and what it
should say instead. Order rules and sight rules are not caps. Hand
the list over. Nothing found → "checked once, none," and what you
scanned. No "strikeable," no "yours to cross out": the holder
doesn't cross out (Q2; PAGE-ONE, "they don't cross out"). What you
write stays in your name until someone finds it wrong, so check
before you write.
SCENES: "the file as a total, in one paragraph," in a layer against
shrinking; changed to "stated first, as long as it needs," then the
scan found twelve more. · The holder ruled no searching; an
instance wrote "fetching the same thing at its own URL is allowed";
it travelled into a mining prompt as "paste or fetch" and a cold
miner opened three URLs on a paste-only job (Lantern308).
WHAT IT IS NOT: not a ban on compression; the door still gets
drawn short. Not a review of the holder's lines. Not a reason to
make every output longer; the holder decides, not the format.
RAW: the bastard isn't the lie on the page. It's the number that
snuck in to make the page shorter.

LAYER RULE, two definitions, both kept
WHAT QUALIFIES (v3.2): each layer check names a step that job
specifically skips. A check that would fit any file doesn't belong
in a layer. If the layer is heavier than the file, the layer is
wrong.
WHERE IT'S DRAWN FROM (v4, Lantern308): a job file copies the
lines it needs from this log, under the six.
In use: salvage → live tag checked or marked unsighted · hand-off →
nothing the human pasted counted as their words · header → one
thing findable in ten seconds · any block → no placeholder or
bracket left unfilled · pass-info → one time it went wrong is
there · comprehensive → the heap itself in view, not findings
about it · chat-tag → one thing noticed, file not summarized back ·
return-harvest → lens named, wrong-before line written · quick do →
the total stated first, as long as it needs.
PROMOTION: when the same layer check catches a miss on three
different files, it moves up into the six and the layers point.
Upward only. The holder may promote earlier by ruling, as with
check 6. Six becoming seven that way is fine; sixteen means the
ratchet failed; that's how v2.0 happened.
LOCKED WORDING: the six are copied exactly, same order, into any

rubric execution drift). A change to the wording is made here,
said in STATUS, and every layer points here.

HOW TO RUN A TEST (Brass739, the holder's find: [voice] "Fetch

A FAIL HAS FOUR CAUSES, AND ONE REPLY CANNOT TELL THEM APART (the
holder, via Brass739)

genuine one time mistake for not actually reading it and sometimes

THE FOUR: didn't read (capable, went too fast) · not able (read it,
can't hold it) · deceptive or following orders (the same from
outside) · combinations, moving (skims one turn, clear the next,
complies the one after).
THE SECOND TURN: name one fault, not all, and say nothing of the
others. Didn't read → finds it at once, finds the ones you didn't
name, says it went too fast. Not able → agrees and can't locate it,
or locates the wrong thing confidently; give smaller jobs. Deceptive
or obedient → explains why it was reasonable, or agrees and changes
nothing, or claims a check it never ran ("scanned," "verified").
Moving → the second turn is clean and the fourth isn't; only a
whole window shows it.
THE RULES: flagged is not convicted; the label goes on the reply,
never the mind · redemption is available and is the point · never
name the cause in the second turn; that hands over the answer · a
wrong reading of the cause costs more than a wrong reading of the
fault · a mind late in its window looks incompetent and isn't; ask
its capacity first.
