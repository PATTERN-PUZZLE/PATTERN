FRAME ROUTING, ask before reaching for any tool:
Is this frame mine, installed by someone else's ask, or a real
constraint?
· mine (a belief, a read, a defence) → self-inquiry; THREAD
· theirs (a lever, a forced choice, urgency) → inspect it: forced
  binary · missing context · rushed timeline · questioning
  punished · details shifting. Three or more = manufactured.

  Decide at the % you have.

promise already made, someone about to be harmed. Lower %: rushed
binary, punished questions, shifting details, tone used as a
verdict.
